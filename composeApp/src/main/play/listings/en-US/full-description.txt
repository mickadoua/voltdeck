Voltdeck brings the Bluetooth readings of the 12/24 V electrical system of your van, boat or off-grid cabin into one app.

One app instead of one per brand. A history that stays on your phone.

WHAT YOU SEE

• An overview: solar production, battery (charge %, voltage, current, power, temperature, cells, cycles), consumption and estimated runtime.
• Statistics by day, week and month: energy produced and used, battery charge curve, minimums and maximums, comparison with the previous period. An accessible table backs up every chart.
• Dark and light themes, with key numbers readable at a glance. The interface is currently in French.

READ-ONLY

Voltdeck reads, it never writes. The app sends your devices no command that changes a setting, a mode or a state.

NO DATA LEAVES YOUR PHONE

• No account, no sign-up.
• The app has no permission to access the Internet.
• No ads, no analytics, no crash reports sent.
• History is stored on the device. Victron keys are kept in Android's secure keystore.

SUPPORTED EQUIPMENT

• Victron Energy (Instant Readout, passive listening to Bluetooth advertisements, no connection): SmartSolar MPPT, SmartShunt and BMV, Orion Smart DC-DC. You enter each device's key, shown in VictronConnect.
• Lithium batteries with a JBD BMS, including Ultimatron and other brands.

Voltdeck is young: these drivers are validated against reference data and a simulator, and feedback from real hardware is welcome. More brands can be added.

SCREEN-OFF COLLECTION

For a history without gaps, Voltdeck can read your devices in the background. A notification stays visible meanwhile, with a button to stop. Collection resumes after the phone restarts.

TRY IT WITHOUT CONNECTING ANYTHING

Demo mode shows the app with simulated values, with no Bluetooth and no permission.

OPEN SOURCE

Voltdeck's code is open (Apache-2.0 licence). You can check what it does.
